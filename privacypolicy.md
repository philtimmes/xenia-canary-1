Privacy Policy for Nexia360
Last Updated: July 5, 2025
The Nexia360 project ("Project") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Nexia360 software, documentation, wiki, online sessions, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required by law.
1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information when you use the Services:

Usage Data: Information about your interactions with the Services, such as IP addresses, device information (e.g., operating system, hardware details), and log data (e.g., access times, error reports) to help us maintain and improve the Services.
User Contributions: Information you voluntarily provide, such as contributions to the Project’s GitHub repository, wiki, or Discord server (e.g., usernames, code submissions, or messages).
Online Session Data: Technical data related to netplay sessions, such as connection details or session identifiers, to facilitate multiplayer functionality.

We do not collect personally identifiable information (e.g., names, email addresses, or payment details) unless voluntarily provided by you through community platforms like Discord or GitHub.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

To operate, maintain, and improve the Services, including the emulator, netplay features, and REST API.
To troubleshoot technical issues and ensure the stability of online sessions.
To analyze usage patterns and optimize the Project’s performance and compatibility.
To facilitate community interactions, such as processing contributions to the repository or wiki.
To comply with legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests from authorities.

3. Non-Sharing of Information
We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a court order, subpoena, or other legal process.
Project Maintenance: We may use aggregated, anonymized data internally to improve the Services, but this data cannot be used to identify you.

Any information you voluntarily share on public platforms (e.g., GitHub or Discord) is subject to those platforms’ respective privacy policies.
4. Data Storage and Security

Storage: Data collected by the Services is stored securely on servers managed by the Project maintainers or trusted service providers (e.g., Heroku for netplay sessions). We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.
Security: We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices and Rights
You have the following rights regarding your information:

Access and Control: You may review or delete your contributions to the Project’s GitHub repository or Discord server, subject to those platforms’ policies.
Opt-Out: You may choose not to participate in online sessions or community platforms to limit data collection.
Data Requests: If you believe we hold personal information about you, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable laws.

6. Cookies and Tracking
The Services do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. However, our hosting provider for online sessions (e.g., Heroku) may use temporary session identifiers for technical purposes, which are not used to track you across other sites.
7. Third-Party Services
The Project interacts with third-party platforms, such as GitHub, Discord, and Heroku. These platforms have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their policies before using their services.
8. Children’s Privacy
The Services are not directed to individuals under 13 years old. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), we will take steps to delete it as soon as possible.
9. International Users
The Services are hosted in the United States and subject to U.S. law. If you access the Services from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. By using the Services, you consent to this transfer and processing.
10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on the Project’s GitHub repository or related platforms, with the “Last Updated” date revised. Your continued use of the Services after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
11. Contact Information
For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Project maintainers via the Project’s GitHub repository or Discord server.
By using the Nexia360 project, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.
